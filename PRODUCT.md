# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A landlord or small property owner managing a handful of rooms in Vietnam. They use this app once a month — usually on a phone or laptop — to calculate bills for electricity, water, and services, then share a QR-enabled invoice with each tenant. They are not tech-savvy; they care about speed and accuracy, not features.

## Product Purpose

A monthly bill calculator for Vietnamese rental rooms. It takes meter readings and service fees, computes totals per room, and generates a printable invoice with a VietQR bank transfer code. Success is a landlord who can produce a correct, shareable invoice in under two minutes without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Warm, clear, dependable. Like a trusted neighbor helping with paperwork — not a bank app, not a startup. Feels personal and human, not corporate or playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS blue-gradient dashboards (Salesforce, Notion-lite clones) — too corporate
- Spreadsheet-heavy UIs (dense tables, Excel-like rows) — intimidating, not approachable
- Cute cartoon apps (bubbly icons, pastel toy aesthetics) — not serious enough for financial data
- Dark/terminal interfaces — alienates everyday Vietnamese users

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the hero.** Every layout decision puts the figures front and center. Labels serve numbers, not the other way around.
2. **One task at a time.** Each screen has one clear job. No sidebars, no parallel flows, no distractions.
3. **Trust through clarity.** The user must understand at a glance what was calculated and why. Ambiguity erodes trust in a financial tool.
4. **Mobile-first but not mobile-only.** Most users pull this up on a phone; the experience must feel native at 390px and still work gracefully at 1024px.
5. **Vietnamese context first.** Currency formatting, bank transfer conventions, and the VietQR flow are core to the product — not an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text)
- Touch targets ≥ 44px for mobile usability
- No reliance on color alone to convey meaning (important for color-blind users reading bill breakdowns)
